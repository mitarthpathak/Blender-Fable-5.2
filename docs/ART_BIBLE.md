# Art Bible

One page. Everything built for this project should be checked against it.

## Setting

**Halden Ridge**, a fictional small town in the north-eastern United States,
**late 1990s**. A mill town past its best: the mill has closed, half the
street is empty, the rest is hanging on. Inspired by classic foggy-town
survival horror, but every building, name and sign is our own. Nothing is
copied from a real game or a real place.

## Time and weather

Night, late autumn. Thick ground fog, cold and damp. No wind. Wet surfaces,
bare trees, leaves on the ground. Visibility is 20 to 30 metres outdoors.

## Palette

Muted grey-green-brown. Natural, believable colours: nothing cartoonish,
nothing oversaturated, no pure black or pure white.

| Source | Colour | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moon | cold blue | key light, through windows and fog |
| Sodium streetlights | dull orange | outdoors, and spilling in at the front |
| Indoor lamps | warm | small accents only, never the main light |
| Flashlight (later) | neutral white | the player's main light |

Albedo stays in a physical range: darkest about 4%, brightest about 80%.

## Architecture

Two-storey wooden houses in painted **clapboard**, paint faded and peeling.
Covered front **porches** with steps and railings, **screen doors**, sash
windows with plain board casings. Gutters and downpipes, stained where
water runs. Picket and chain-link fences, mailboxes on posts, telephone
poles with sagging wires, cracked concrete paths, patchy grass.

## Interiors

Lived in for decades and not kept up. Patterned **wallpaper**, faded and
lifting; dark **wood panelling**; worn **carpet** and **linoleum**; a CRT
television; stained ceilings; peeling paint; water damage under windows and
below the bathroom. Clutter is ordinary and specific: post, mugs, boxes,
a coat on a hook.

## Realism

2026 standards on a 1990s subject: PBR materials with albedo, normal and
roughness on every surface; wear, grime, stains and edge damage from decals
and texture variation, never flat colour; baked global illumination;
neutral tone mapping (AgX), no strong colour grade.

The **Low** graphics preset must still read clearly on integrated graphics:
it keeps normal maps, baked lighting, decals and mipmaps, and drops only the
expensive effects.

## Character

Undecided. For now a neutral, realistic adult in plain clothes: jacket,
jeans, boots. No stylised proportions.
